Method: 构建集成式平台：后端在预设城市边界内按 1 km 网格轮询查询点、去重并存储公开内容；前端支持采集任务管理、交互浏览、相邻区域并列比较和数据导出。随后在一个城市部署 23 天，并通过连续 21 次重复查询开展饱和度测试。

Result: 在 2,740 个查询点上共获得 515,364 条唯一公开记录；饱和度测试显示，返回记录中 94.8% 已存在于存储库，说明重复查询的新增收益较低。作者发布遵循 CC BY 4.0 的隐私保护聚合数据集，且平台可通过替换网格坐标和边界多边形迁移到其他城市。

Conclusion: SnapScope 证明了可在城市尺度上对 Snap Map 公开、带地理标签的短时效内容进行系统化采集、管理与交互探索，并以聚合数据共享降低隐私风险。

Abstract: Snapchat's Snap Map is an ephemeral stream of geotagged public video and image stories, but the platform provides no documented API, no prior work describes a reproducible system for collecting this data at city scale, and no tool exists for managing and exploring the collected data interactively. We present SnapScope, an integrated platform that pairs a back-end collection pipeline with a web-based front end for scraper management, interactive data exploration, side-by-side neighborhood comparison, and data export. We deploy the platform over Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, collecting 515,364 unique public snaps across 23 days on a 1 km grid of 2,740 query points. A saturation probe over 21 consecutive runs shows that 94.8% of returned observations are duplicates of already-stored records. We provide a privacy-safe aggregate dataset under CC BY 4.0. The platform is city-agnostic and redeployable by substituting grid coordinates and boundary polygons.
