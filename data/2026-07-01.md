Motivation: 传统 CRDT 假设参与方是良性的；在拜占庭场景中，常依赖身份过滤来判定更新有效性。但当原本可信参与者事后被攻陷时，简单地回溯排除其更新可能破坏因果依赖并影响后续计算，因此需要更细粒度的信任控制。

Method: 基于确定性重构机制，将来源身份（如公钥）过滤与单个更新的语义级策略判断结合，使副本能够在保留已接受更新的同时，对后续或特定更新进行选择性纳入或排除。

Result: 该方法声称能够保持因果一致性，并支持应用层策略对更新影响进行精确控制，从而更灵活地处理去中心化 CRDT 系统中的恶意或故障行为。

Conclusion: 本文提出将身份信任与内容信任解耦的细粒度信任模型，用于提升拜占庭环境下 CRDT 系统在节点被攻陷后的鲁棒性与可控性。

Abstract: Conflict-free Replicated Data Types (CRDTs) provide strong eventual consistency without coordination, but classical approaches assume benign participants. In Byzantine settings, convergence is typically enforced through agreement on update validity, often relying on identity-based filtering. However, such approaches struggle in post-compromise scenarios, where a previously correct participant becomes malicious: retroactive exclusion of its updates may break causal dependencies and invalidate subsequent computations. In this paper, we decouple identity-based trust from content-based trust and introduce a fine-grained trust model that combines both dimensions. Building on deterministic reconstruction, our approach allows replicas to preserve previously accepted updates while enabling selective inclusion or exclusion based on both the originating identity (e.g., public keys) and the semantics of individual updates. Trust decisions can incorporate application-level policies, enabling precise control over the impact of each update on the system state. Our approach preserves causal consistency and enables robust and flexible handling of both Byzantine and faulty behavior in decentralized CRDT systems.
