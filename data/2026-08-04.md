Motivation: LEO卫星具有移动性，导致地面源与卫星之间的链路在连通与中断间交替变化，而源节点无法预知当前连通状态。如果盲目发送更新，可能在离线时白白消耗能量，影响信息新鲜度。因此需要研究在不确定链路下，如何通过探测机制提升状态更新的时效性。

Method: 结合球面随机几何与半马尔可夫分析，联合刻画卫星连通状态与源节点能量缓冲区的耦合演化；在此基础上推导时间平均AoI的解析式，并通过将能量过程中的瞬时连通状态替换为长期处于连接状态的概率，构造低复杂度近似模型。

Result: 理论分析得到时间平均AoI表达式，并验证近似方法在能量约束较弱或链路间歇性较强时具有较高精度。数值结果显示，探测机制相较于盲传输能显著降低AoI，尤其在卫星部署稀疏、译码要求严格或能量采集不足的场景下效果突出。

Conclusion: 本文针对LEO卫星辅助的能量采集IoT网络，建立了AoI时效性分析框架，并给出了时间平均AoI的解析表达及低复杂度近似方法。结果表明，探测后传输机制可有效降低AoI，尤其在卫星稀疏、接收门限较高或能量采集受限时优势更明显。

Abstract: We analyze the timeliness of status-update delivery in a low Earth orbit (LEO) satellite-assisted energy-harvesting Internet of Things network using the Age of Information (AoI) metric. A ground source harvests ambient energy and sends status updates to a remote destination through LEO satellites. Because of satellite mobility, source-to-satellite connectivity alternates between on and off periods whose durations depend on the satellite-ground geometry. The source does not know the connectivity state a priori and therefore employs a probe-before-transmission mechanism: it first expends one energy unit to sense satellite availability and transmits an update only after a successful probe. We combine spherical stochastic geometry with semi-Markov analysis to characterize the coupled evolution of satellite connectivity and the source energy buffer, and derive an analytical expression for the time-average AoI. We then develop a lower-complexity approximation by replacing the instantaneous connectivity state in the energy process with the long-term on-state probability. The resulting approximation is accurate when the energy constraint is weak or satellite connectivity is highly intermittent. Numerical results show that probing can substantially reduce AoI relative to blind transmission by preventing energy expenditure during off periods, particularly under sparse satellite deployment, stringent decoding requirements, or limited energy harvesting.
